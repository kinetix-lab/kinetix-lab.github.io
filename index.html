What Kinetix POS does

Many of these features already work in development builds. The rest are on the roadmap for the first release.

In progress being built and tested now Planned on the roadmap
In progress

Fast register

A touch-friendly ordering screen for counter, dine-in and takeaway, with modifiers, options and live totals.

In progress

Menus, deals and promotions

Menu items with option groups, meal deals built from your menu, and scheduled promotions.

In progress

Kitchen display (KDS)

Orders go straight to kitchen stations and the pass. Changes made after an order is sent show up as clear notices.

In progress

Till and cash management

Till sessions with an opening float, a cash ledger, blind counts, variance approval, and X and Z reports.

In progress

Payments

Cash and split tenders, change, and refunds now. Card payments through integrated terminals come next.

In progress

Inventory

Stock levels for each store, kept accurate by recipes and reservations, with stock counts and a full movement history.

In progress

Purchasing and receiving

Purchase orders, supplier packaging, and goods receiving that updates stock as deliveries arrive.

In progress

Product catalogue

Head office keeps one catalogue of products, variants and SKUs, which every store then sells from.

In progress

Multi-store and franchise

Several businesses and stores on one platform, with role-based access for head office, owners and managers.

In progress

UK VAT

Tax is calculated on the server, and purchase tax is kept separate from sales tax.

Planned

Delivery platform integration

Orders from food delivery apps come into the same kitchen and stock flow as every other order.

Planned

Devices and peripherals

Receipt and kitchen printers, cash drawers, scanners and scales, with devices registered and paired to each store.

Planned

Self-service kiosk and customer display

Customers can order at a kiosk, and a second screen shows them their order and total.

Planned

Reporting

Sales, stock and till reports for each store and across the whole business.

Planned

Staff PINs and manager overrides

Staff switch quickly on shared terminals, and voids, refunds and discounts need an audited manager approval.
